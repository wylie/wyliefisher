This week we finally made it to a Family Dungeons & Dragons night at WellPlayed, and it was awesome! Super fun, the DM was great and understanding that we were very new. The other two players were also nice. One was very young and adorable, especially when they had to kill an enemy. The other was very knowledgeable about the game and helped the DM with some stuff. It was very fun, and I highly recommend it for people who want to learn how to play D&D.

We have a new bookstore in our town! It's a romance bookstore, and while I don't read a ton of romance books, a ton of the ones I do read end up having some romance in them. You can find that anywhere!
